01 • THE PROBLEM

Conveyor failure
starts before the shutdown.

Belt joints, tracking, tension, loading and progressive wear can develop abnormal signatures before a visible production failure. The objective is to detect those signatures early and connect them to an actionable maintenance response.

01

Joint rupture

Progressive joint degradation can become a sudden belt failure.

02

Misalignment

Tracking deviation increases wear, vibration and operational risk.

03

Overloading

Abnormal load and tension can accelerate belt and joint damage.

04

Reactive maintenance

Late detection increases downtime, maintenance effort and spillage.

02 • THE SOLUTION

From raw signals
to maintenance action.

ConveySentinel continuously combines physical sensor telemetry and visual inspection to estimate conveyor health, identify developing faults and support PLC/SCADA intervention.

02ProcessRFID • Encoder • TMS320C6748 DSP • FFT features
